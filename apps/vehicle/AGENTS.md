# AGENTS.md: apps/vehicle

`sat-teleop-vehicle` (L3) is the companion computer's entry point. It stays thin: config loading and wiring over `sat-teleop-media` (video) and `sat-teleop-control` (MAVLink).

- `sat-teleop-vehicle probe-camera` lists the video sources GStreamer sees and their modes.
- `sat-teleop-vehicle run --config <toml> --limits <toml> --ladder <toml> [--start-rung <n>] --metrics-addr <addr> [--token-file <file>] [--job-token-file <file>]` validates the files, runs the sender at the ladder's start rung (or `--start-rung`, counted from the best; the testbed passes 0) and the adaptation loop (`sat_teleop_media::adapt`) under `sat_teleop_media::supervise` and the MAVLink bridge with its control endpoint (`sat_teleop_control::vehicle::run`) on threads, and serves `GET /metrics` on the main thread. The adaptation loop sets the sender's rung and, from the uplink budget, the bridge's telemetry cap; its budget reserves the control traffic (probes and challenges at their largest sizes on the wire, or every reply as measured when more) and telemetry's share of what SITL offers (`[telemetry]` `offered_percent`, between `essential_bytes_per_s` and `bytes_per_s`) before video. Every route of the metrics endpoint, the test-only hooks included, needs the bearer token of `--token-file` (without one it listens on 127.0.0.1 only); the relay's job API token is another file, `--job-token-file` (without it the job API is not used), so the metrics token never leaves the vehicle. If the metrics endpoint cannot bind, the process exits.
- Config: `testbed/config/vehicle.toml` (`destination` and `[source]` for video (`test-pattern`, `file` or `ingest`: `listen` and `from` addresses, RTP/H.264; `testbed/config/vehicle-ingest.toml`), `feedback` the relay's feedback address, `debug_hooks` (default false; testbed builds only), `encoder` (`x264`, the default, or `hardware`), `[mavlink]` with the `vehicle_id` (the name every signature is made for; `ground.toml` has the same), the `autopilot` (`tcpout:<loopback ip>:<port>` or `serial:<device>:<baud>`), `relay` and `control` addresses and the `controllers` that may take the lease; a TCP autopilot off loopback or an empty `controllers` is refused, and control reaches the autopilot only once it has reported the SITL marker parameter, otherwise the logging sink takes it, `crates/control/AGENTS.md`). `[source]` is also `v4l2` (`device`, `format`, `width`, `height`, `fps`) or `libcamera` (`width`, `height`, `fps`) on a Pi, `docs/pi.md`; `deploy/vehicle/vehicle.toml` is that config; limits: `testbed/limits.toml` (`[telemetry]` `bytes_per_s`, `offered_percent`, `essential_bytes_per_s`, `[control]` lifetimes, `[adapt]` thresholds; the other tables are the ground's); ladder: `testbed/ladder.toml` (`keyframe_interval_ms`, `start`, `[[rung]]` with `group`, `width`, `height`, `fps`, `bitrate_kbit`). Unknown keys in them are rejected. In the testbed, metrics listen on `192.168.233.10:9100` and the control endpoint on `10.77.0.2:7700`.
- Retained snapshots: the sender's `[snapshot]` branch hands each JPEG to a writer thread over a queue of 2 (a full queue drops the snapshot, counted); the writer puts it in the `sat_teleop_detail` store (`[store]` of the vehicle config: `dir`, `max_bytes`, `max_age_s`, `min_free_bytes`, `gap_after_ms`; `[snapshot]` needs it). A restart rescans the directory. `detail = "http://host:port"` is the relay's job API: a thread polls it and works the jobs (`sat_teleop_detail::vehicle`) when `[retrieval] allowance_bytes_per_s` of the limits is above 0, and `retrieval_conditions` of the adaptation loop says whether it may send. While a job runs the adaptation loop reserves the allowance in its budget (`Feedback::retrieval`). The source of the testbed's test pattern is 1280x720 so that snapshots and crops have a real size.
- Sightings: while `ground_heard` is false the detector thread records each person or vehicle it finds (with its kind), with the frame's UNIX capture time and the bridge's fresh position, in `VehicleStats::sightings` (`note_sightings`; no fresh position, nothing recorded). The same `ground_heard` test (`heard`) is handed to the bridge, which tells the sightings as `STATUSTEXT` once the ground is heard, and to the snapshot writer. The retrieval loop lists them for `just sightings`.
- Regional video: the limits are `[region]` of the limits file (`RegionSettings`, `sat-teleop-media`); `[region]` of the vehicle config has `detector = { model, input, kinds }` (the detector, off by default; `kinds` is a list of person, bicycle, car, motorcycle, bus, truck, boat, default `["person"]`, an unknown name or an empty list is refused at load) `follow` (default false: the pilot's box is followed from frame to frame, on a branch of its own and a thread of its own, `follow_things`, which feeds each frame to `sat_teleop_policy::follow::Follower` and tells the cell where the target is or that it is lost; the tracker's limits are in `[region]` of the limits file) and `soften_factor` (absent or 1: nothing is softened and the pilot's box is not kept; 2 to 4 soften; more is refused). Detections are outlined whenever the detector runs. The model loads (`load_detector`) before the sender starts; the detector thread (`find_things`) runs `sat_teleop_detect::Detector` on the newest frame the sender offers. A model that does not load is logged and shown as `detector.state` `failed: <cause>` in the metrics, the sender builds no detector branch, and the video is unaffected. The sender, the detector and the control loop share one `RegionCell` (`bridge.control.region`). Metrics add `region` and `detector` (`crates/media/AGENTS.md`).
- Debug hooks, for testing only: `POST /video/pause`, `/video/freeze` and `/video/resume` on the metrics endpoint set the sender's source mode (`sat-teleop-media`, `SourceMode`) and answer `{"mode": ...}`; `POST /snapshots/slow` makes every snapshot write wait 3 s first (storage slower than the snapshot rate) and `/snapshots/normal` ends it. With `debug_hooks` unset or false they answer 403 and change nothing. The hooks (`src/hooks.rs`), `debug_hooks` and `[mavlink] sitl_in_real_mode` compile only with the `testbed` cargo feature, which the testbed's runtime image enables; a binary built without it refuses a config that names `debug_hooks` or `sitl_in_real_mode`. In real mode the loader refuses `debug_hooks` without `sitl_in_real_mode`. Never build a release artifact with the feature.
- Metrics JSON: the rung `requested` (`rung`, `seq` counting the requests from 1, `unix_ms`) and `applied` (`rung`, the `seq` of the request it applied, the first `frame_id` stamped in its shape, `after_request_ms` on the monotonic clock, `unix_ms`), the source `mode` (`live`, `paused`, `frozen`), `frames_stamped`, `stamp_failures`, `ingest` (the ingest source's packets, refusals and decode time, zero otherwise), `frames_encoded`, `rtp_packets`, `rtp_bytes`, pipeline health (`runs`, `running`, `last_error`), and `mavlink`: `telemetry_cap` (the telemetry cap now, frame bytes per second), `sitl` (link state: `up`, `ups`, `last_error`), `sitl_bytes` (everything read from SITL), `telemetry` (SITL to the relay) and `gcs` (the relay to SITL, through the gate), each with `messages_in`, `bytes_in`, `messages_out`, `bytes_out`, `dropped` by reason and `receive_errors`, `gcs_refused`: what the gate dropped, counted by message name, by `<message> <command>` for refused commands, or `undecodable <id>`, and `control`: `target` (`autopilot` or `logging_sink`: where control frames go now), `mode` (`sitl` or `real`), `interlocks` (the failing ones, each with `interlock` and `reason`), `clock` (`source`, `bound_ms`, `injected`), `key_errors`, `verified`, `verify_us_total` and `verify_us_max` (envelope signature checks and their time), `sitl_marker`, `probes`, `sunk` (frames the sink took), the control endpoint's socket (`link`), the frames its envelopes and refreshes wrote to SITL (`sitl`, with drops), `epoch`, the unexpired `lease` (`id`, `holder`) or `null`, `grants`, `challenges`, `replies` and `reply_bytes` (every answer sent to a controller, payload bytes), `applied` by kind (`keepalive`, `liveness`, `continuous`, `discrete`), `repeated` (retried transactions answered from the cache), `unsupported`, `refreshed`, `reply_failed` (answers to a controller that could not be sent), `rejected` by reason, `last_continuous` and `last_discrete` (`sent`, `received`, `applied` in UNIX ms) and `last_refresh_unix_ms`; and `adapt`: the current `rung` (0 the best) and its `group`, `probes`, `send_failed`, `answers`, `refused`, `receive_errors`, `changes` (rung changes, drains included) and the `recent_changes` (`to`, `group`, `reason`, `unix_ms`, `at_ms` since the loop started on the monotonic clock, the last 16; `reason` is the controller's, `drain` for a cut that pauses the video, `resume` when it runs again), `draining`, `rtt_ms`, `min_rtt_ms`, `queue_delay_ms`, the loss window's `loss_lost` and `loss_packets`, `uplink_bytes_per_s` (measured, on the wire), `capacity_bytes_per_s` (while it bounds the climb), `telemetry_cap`, and the loop's health; `snapshots` (`taken`, `dropped`, `unidentified`), `retained` (the store: `count`, `bytes`, `oldest_ms`, `newest_ms`, `written`, `evicted`, `gaps`, `largest_gap_ms`, the start `recovery` with `kept`, `tmp_removed`, `ignored`; the writer's `write_failed` and `write_delay_ms`) and `retrieval` (`state`, the `hold` reason, `jobs_done`, `jobs_failed`, `jobs_cancelled`, `jobs_expired`, `chunks_sent`, `chunk_failures`, `payload_bytes`, `wire_bytes`, `holds` (times it began to hold back) and `held_ms`).

Use `anyhow` with `.context()`. Pipelines belong in `sat-teleop-media`, MAVLink in `sat-teleop-control`. Documented in `docs/testbed.md`.
