# AGENTS.md: crates/media

`sat-teleop-media` (L2) is what the drone adds to the video path. It builds on `sat-teleop-video` (which knows nothing of drones) through its two extension points, the sender's frame hook and taps and the output's decoration, and on `sat-teleop-policy` for the regions, kinds, softening, aim ring and inset logic. It owns regional softening and outlines with the detector and tracker frames, the landing tag feed, the predictive display and the detail inset the ground draws, and what retrieval asks of adaptation. The apps wire it to the video pipelines. The name stays: it is still the drone's use of the picture, and renaming would touch every app and lab import for no gain. See the root `AGENTS.md`, `crates/video/AGENTS.md`, `docs/testbed.md` and `docs/measurement.md`.

## Wiring

- `hooks::sender_hooks(regional, tags)` builds the sender's `Hooks`: the `Regional` frame hook plus the taps of the detector, the tracker (when they run) and the tag feed.
- The ground builds a `Decoration` around `decorate::draw` and `decorate::NOTE`, and serves `InsetCount` next to the output's counters as `video.inset_frames`.
- `retrieval::conditions` reads the vehicle's adaptation (`AdaptStats`) into the `Conditions` retrieval asks (`sat_teleop_policy::retrieval::permit`); the video crate only reserves a flow (`reserved` in the budget and the controller) and does not know what uses it.

## Pipelines

- `sender` regional video (`region.rs`; limits in `[region]` of `testbed/limits.toml`, validated by `RegionSettings::validate`; `soften_factor` is in `[region]` of the vehicle config, 1-4, absent or 1 is off and `validate_soften_factor` refuses the rest. With it off the pilot's box has a ttl of 0 (never kept or confirmed) and detections are still outlined; `softened_frames` counts only frames that were softened):
  - Order in the stamp probe: read `capture_ms`, then `Regional::shape` softens the picture outside `RegionCell::regions()` (`policy::soften`, plane by plane) and outlines the detections in their kind's colour (`Kind::yuv`; 2 px at 640 wide, scaled with the width) and tags each with its kind's letter at the top-left corner (pixel size the outline's thickness), then the stamp is drawn. The stamp is never softened. The pilot's box is not drawn; QGroundControl draws it.
  - The regions come from `RegionCell`, which the vehicle's control loop shares: the pilot's box, the detections and the limits. The control loop confirms to the ground the pilot box `RegionCell::pilot_in_use()` returns, so a box the sender drops (too large, crowded out, softening off) is never reported as applied. Never compute the regions anywhere else.
  - Detector branch (only when `[region] detector` is in the vehicle config and its model loaded: the vehicle loads it before the sender starts, and builds no branch otherwise): the tee's third branch, `queue leaky=downstream max-size-buffers=1` with `copy_frames` thinning to `detector_fps` and copying, `videoscale` -> `videoconvert` -> RGB `input` x `input` capsfilter -> `appsink max-buffers=1 drop=true sync=false`. The callback copies the frame into a `DetectorFrame` and `offer`s it, replacing the one still waiting: a busy detector skips to the newest frame and never holds the live video. The detector runs in `sat-teleop-vehicle` (`sat-teleop-detect` is a sibling crate that media cannot depend on); it publishes with `Regional::detected`. A model that does not load sets `detector.state` to `failed: <cause>` and the video goes on.
  - Follow branch (only with `Regional::new(.., follow: true)`, `[region] follow` of the vehicle config): the tee's fourth branch, `queue leaky=downstream max-size-buffers=1` with `copy_frames` thinning to `follow_fps`, `videoscale` -> `videoconvert` -> `GRAY8` at `follow::WIDTH` x `follow::HEIGHT` -> `appsink max-buffers=1 drop=true sync=false`. The callback offers the luma (`FollowFrame`, only if it is exactly one picture) to `Regional::next_follow_frame`, the same newest-wins `Handoff` the detector has, so a slow tracker skips frames and never holds the live video. The tracker runs in `sat-teleop-vehicle`. The followed box (`RegionCell::followed`) is outlined last, in `follow::YUV` with an F tab, a colour no kind has. `[region]` of the limits file adds `follow_fps` (1-30, default 10), `follow_min_score` (above 0, at most 1, default 0.6) and `follow_lost_frames` (1-60, default 3), validated by `RegionSettings::validate`.
  - Tag branch (only when `[landing] target` is on, `tags::TagFeed` passed to `sender::run`): a tee branch of its own, `queue leaky=downstream max-size-buffers=1` with `copy_frames` thinning to `tag_fps`, `videoscale` -> `videoconvert` -> GRAY8 capsfilter of width `tag_width` (the height keeps the picture's shape) -> `appsink max-buffers=1 drop=true sync=false`. The callback packs the rows without padding into a `TagFrame` (with `Instant::now()` as its capture time: the frame has just left the tee) and `offer`s it, replacing the one still waiting, so a busy tag thread skips to the newest frame and the live video never waits. The tag thread is in `sat-teleop-vehicle`.
  - Metrics: `region` (`active` in the last frame, `softened_frames`, `shape_failures` (frames that could not be shaped and went on with their stamp), `soften` stage percentiles) `detector` (`boxes`, the active detections with their kind; `state`: `off`, `running` or `failed: <cause>`, `runs`, `found` (people and vehicles), `ms` stage percentiles) and `follow` (`state`: `off`, `idle` or `following`; `frames`, `score` of the last match, `lost` and `last_lost` (`score`, `edge` or `flat`), `target` as x0, y0, x1, y1, `ms` stage percentiles).

- Taps: the detector, tracker and tag taps are `sender::Tap`s that `Regional` and `TagFeed` own (`taps()`, `tap()`); their consumers turn a `TapFrame` into a `DetectorFrame`, `FollowFrame` or `TagFrame` (`at_ms` is the cell's time minus how long ago the frame left the tee). `next_follow_frame` skips a buffer that is not exactly the picture the tracker works on, `TagFeed::next_frame` removes row padding.
- `decorate` (the ground): With `[camera] hfov_deg` (`CameraConfig`) and a `Predictive` the output also draws `sat_teleop_policy::predict::mark` into each current frame (aim ring and horizon, `mark::draw`) right after the state stamp and before the overlay, so the label is drawn over it; the inputs are read per frame and any missing input draws nothing. The detail inset (`inset`: `Image`, a retained crop decoded with limits and scaled once to `sat_teleop_policy::inset::fit`'s size as I420, and `DetailCell`, the newest `Image` with the age it arrived with, aged by the time held) is drawn by `Pipeline::push` after the ring: `inset::place` decides from the frame's state and the crop's age, so only a `Live` or `LowRate` frame and a crop under 30 s old get it, and the crop's age rides in the buffer's offset (`NO_INSET` otherwise) to a probe that sets a second `textoverlay`, top right, silent without an inset, to `DETAIL <age> s` in the live label's colour, in the same streaming thread as the live label; `inset_frames` counts the frames that had it. Never draw it into another state, over the stamp rows or without its label.
- `retrieval::conditions` is the vehicle's adaptation as retrieval needs it to decide; `AdaptSnapshot::reserve_fits` (a flow of the allowance fits beside a normal rung) comes from `Controller::reserve_fits`.

## Invariants

- Everything drawn into a frame is drawn before the stamp (the hook) or after the state stamp and before the labels (the decoration); never over the stamp rows.
- No tap or hook may make the live video wait; a slow reader skips to the newest frame.
