# AGENTS.md: crates/lab

`sat-teleop-lab` (L2) owns impairment profiles, `tc` translation, the router agent, budgets and the `lab check` scenarios (`boot`, `skeleton`, `mavlink`, `control-freshness`, `qgc-status`, `adaptation`, `recording`, `retrieval`, `ingest`, `regional`, `sim-flight`, `sim-mission`) with their results. See the root `AGENTS.md`, `docs/testbed.md` and `docs/measurement.md`.

## Invariants

- `profile.rs` validates everything before the router is touched: ranges, finite percentages, timeline order and combinations. Unknown fields are rejected.
- `tc.rs`, `nft.rs` and `ping.rs` are pure: settings in, `Command` argv out (and, for `ping`, output parsed back). Table-test them. Process execution lives only in `command.rs`.
- Live changes never delete qdiscs: rate is `tc class change`, blackout and restore are `tc qdisc change` on netem. Only `setup`/`teardown` (apply and reset) delete.
- The agent shapes exactly the two resolved interfaces. Interface names are validated (no leading `-`, no `lo`) because they become argv.
- HTTP bodies are capped at 64 KiB, requests are handled one at a time, and no input path may panic.
- Timeline timing uses `Instant`; the thread is stopped (and joined) before any other `tc` runs.
- `check.rs` owns the results contract: a run with any failure fails, anything unmeasured (or no assertion at all) is `incomplete`, never `pass`. Exit codes are 0, 1 and 2.
- A scenario never passes on silence alone: silence assertions (`cgnat`, `ctl_net_carries_no_data`, the forwarding cut) are `incomplete` unless the target was shown to answer, and they need a full stream of unanswered probes. Waiting for a reply retries probe by probe until the deadline; never rely on a single ping or on iputils `-w`, which stops at the first ICMP error. The boot check restores forwarding even when the cut step fails.
- `budget.rs` grades metrics against `perf/budgets.toml`: a calibration budget has no limits and grades `incomplete`; an enforced one needs limits and a rationale; a limit on an unmeasured metric is `incomplete`. Limits come from measured runs, never from the profile a run applied.
- `skeleton.rs` counts only new frames (readable stamp, plausible age 0-60 s, frame id not seen before) towards age and cadence. Its analysis (`Window`, percentiles, `first_fresh`) is pure and table-tested; collection is bounded (64-observation queue, 250 000 stored). The receiver runs under `sat_teleop_media::supervise`; forwarding is restored even when the cut fails, and the cut assertions are `incomplete` without video before the cut.
- `mavlink.rs` acts as a ground station on one UDP socket (the socket and its record of what arrived live in `mavlink/gcs.rs`, which can also repeat a message at a fixed rate or only listen; a record is from the vehicle only from SITL's autopilot, system 1 component 1, and from the camera from component 100, the adapter's camera), with frames read through `sat_teleop_control::frame` and written with `mavlink::write_v2_msg`, never by hand. It keeps a compact record per message (bounded at 500 000). `arm_refused` passes only with evidence that SITL was alive (heartbeats) and that the adapter, holding no lease, denied the command (its counter rose and its `DENIED` arrived); any sign SITL got the command fails. Its verdict is a pure, table-tested function. Forwarding is restored even when the cut fails, and datagrams queued while a phase blocked are read before the cut is timed.
- `control.rs` (`control-freshness`) plays three parts: a second controller on the overlay (`control/intruder.rs`, the only code that writes envelopes by hand, through `sat_teleop_control::envelope`), a ground station of the adapter, and an observer of SITL's telemetry straight from mavlink-router on a thread of its own (so a message is timed when it arrives, and it keeps watching while the adapter is down). Zero applications pass only with positive evidence the vehicle was answering (its applied counts rose by exactly the valid envelopes) and refused each envelope for the rule it breaks; anything applied or an armed heartbeat fails (`untouched_verdict`, table-tested). The adapter is killed by `just lab-check` when the check prints `lab: kill ground`; the check never assumes it happened, it watches the vehicle stop getting setpoints. Every run starts and ends by force-disarming SITL, restoring its stream rates and freeing the lease, so an aborted run cannot poison the next. `control/stop.rs` holds the kill and blackout phases.
- `qgc_status.rs` (`qgc-status`) receives the ground adapter's output through `sat-teleop-media`'s receiver (`Source::Rtp`) with the skeleton's `Capture`, and `qgc_status/camera.rs` asks the adapter's camera what QGC asks it through the `mavlink/gcs.rs` socket. Every run first resumes the vehicle's video, so an aborted run cannot poison the next; every stall (a vehicle hook, or the profile's blackout) is released even when its expected state never showed, the vehicle's video is resumed even when the stall failed, and a hook that cannot be used is `incomplete`. A stall passes only if its states show in order and no frame reads Live after the first one that did not, before the release; `never_falsely_live` holds every Live frame of the run to `stale_after_ms` plus 100 ms by its own stamp.
- `adaptation.rs` (`adaptation`) plays the relay's stream with the skeleton's `Capture`, reads the vehicle's ladder every 0.5 s and the router's counters every second, and grades each downward rate step of the profile's timeline: the offered uplink (the router's bytes from `vehicle_net`, before its queue) below the new cap, and the frame age back to the phase's level. It owns `settle`, which the skeleton and QGC status checks also run before measuring: no rung change for 15 s with no measured uplink bounding the climb, at most 120 s, `incomplete` otherwise. Adaptation uses the full 15 s; the skeleton and QGC status checks use `settle_at_top`, 6 s (the testbed vehicle starts at the top rung, and a ladder there only leaves it by a fall the controller makes within `stale_after_ms` plus `min_change_interval_ms`). The second viewer is compared with one receiver before and after it, so the encoder's own drift after a rung change cancels; a comparison voided by a rung change is `incomplete`, never a pass.
- `recording.rs` (`recording`) mounts the vehicle's snapshot volume and reads the store's limits from the vehicle's config. It waits for the quota to be reached and holds the bytes (directory and the store's own count) to it with the oldest snapshot only ever getting newer; plants a `.tmp` and two names that are no snapshots, has `just lab-check` restart the vehicle's companion (the check prints `lab: restart companion`; without the host it is `incomplete`), and requires the `.tmp` deleted, the others left alone and not indexed, the snapshots on disk indexed, writing resumed and the gap around the restart reported; then makes writes slow (a vehicle hook) and requires snapshots dropped and counted with the live frames unaffected. Writes are set back to normal at the start and the end.
- `control/real_mode.rs` (`real-mode`) plays the ground station and a second controller against the vehicle in real mode. It prints `lab: vehicle-config <file>` for `just lab-check` to restart the companion on `vehicle-real.toml`, `vehicle-real-nokey.toml`, `vehicle-real-noclock.toml` and `vehicle.toml` (the epoch in the vehicle's metrics shows the restart), breaks one interlock at a time through the vehicle's debug hooks and holds a forced arm to be denied, sunk and not applied; a clock bound taken away in flight must leave control reaching the autopilot, while one missing at the start refuses it. The station sends a raw request first so mavlink-router routes SITL's acknowledgements to it. Expects the default testbed and puts it back.
- `retrieval.rs` (`retrieval`) plays a ground (the job API's client) and the stream's receiver. It requests a crop and a snapshot of the frame 8 s ago and compares the answer with the file the volume held (same frame, same size, the rectangle asked for, the snapshot byte for byte); sends the hostile requests (`hostile_cases`: malformed, unknown fields, oversize, out-of-range and empty crops, bad bounds, unknown ids, path traversal, wrong listener, bad chunk index, missing hash, oversize chunk) and the table-filling ones (a fifth job, a third 1 MiB job), all of which must be refused and counted by the relay; cancels a snapshot mid-transfer and times the vehicle's stop; and runs two passes under the profile (re-applied, the ladder settled at the top before each), one without retrieval and one with crop jobs back to back, comparing the live frame age's p50 and p95 and the vehicle's retrieval bytes on the wire against the allowance. Its comparisons and verdicts are in the check, its numbers graded by the budget.
- `regional.rs` (`regional`) needs the vehicle on `vehicle-regional.toml` (or `vehicle-regional-detector.toml`): a one-frame clip of the check's test card (`regional/card.rs`, pure: pad and figure inside the box; figure, lines of 1, 2 and 4 px and 8 px blocks outside) from `/snapshots/card.y4m`, which the check writes to the vehicle's volume, and `debug_hooks`. It scores the ground's output against `sat_teleop_media::card::reference` of the same bytes (SSIM in `regional/ssim.rs`, pure) in three windows: a box tracked through the adapter's camera component as QGroundControl does it (`SET_MESSAGE_INTERVAL` for the tracking status, then `CAMERA_TRACK_RECTANGLE`), the same without it, and the plain picture one rung down (`POST /video/rung/1`). The tracking status must go active only with the box the vehicle confirms (`pilot_in_use`). Its budget is `regional.<profile>` and stays in calibration until a three-run series exists. Not in `gate-full`; run it with `just lab-check regional geo` on a testbed brought up with `VEHICLE_CONFIG=vehicle-regional.toml` (`PI=pi4` or `PI=pi-zero-2w` as for the latency runs).
- `ingest.rs` (`ingest`) reads only the vehicle's metrics, twice, around a window: the feeder container (`testbed/scripts/feeder.sh`) must be running and the vehicle on `vehicle-ingest.toml`, so it is run by `just ingest-check`, not by `gate-full`. The video through the link is `skeleton`'s.
- `control/sim_flight.rs` (`sim-flight`) runs only against the simulated flight (`just sim-check`, ArduCopter in Gazebo; `docs/simulation.md`). `checks` takes the lease, arms with a plain arm (retried while the copter's pre-arm checks refuse; never the forced arm), takes off, flies a leg on `MANUAL_CONTROL`, stops the stream (the copter must brake and hold) and has `just lab-check` kill the adapter (the copter must land and disarm). After the Brake it asks for Loiter with no setpoints flowing: the vehicle's gate must refuse it and the copter stay in Brake (`no_drop_after_brake`). The failsafe verdicts (`brake_verdict`, `land_verdict`) are pure and table-tested over the copter's own reports and judge what happened (it braked, it landed and disarmed, it never descended faster than Land's own limit, 1.25 times ArduPilot 4.7.2's default `WP_SPD_DN`); how soon and how far it sank are graded against `[sim-flight.<profile>]` in `perf/budgets.toml` (`brake_ms`, `brake_sag_m`, `land_ms`, `land_sag_m`, `on_ground_ms`, `video_live_ratio`), and `video_live` takes its minimum Live share from the same budget. It also records the video's age and frame rate during the leg and the simulation's real-time factor, ungraded.
- `control/sim_flight/mission.rs` (`sim-mission`, `just sim-mission <profile> on|off`) reads `mavlink.control.allow_missions` from the vehicle and grades the run as that switch says. On: `checks` uploads a takeoff, three waypoints and a landing the way QGC 5.1.5's `PlanManager` does (a 1500 ms wait for each next request, `MISSION_COUNT` again only before the first request), arms in Guided, sends `MAV_CMD_MISSION_START`, has `just lab-check` kill the adapter once the copter reports the first waypoint reached, and requires the rest of the waypoints reached with the copter in Auto throughout, then on the ground and disarmed. Off (`vehicle-sim.toml`): the upload, `MISSION_START` and Auto must each be answered denied and counted (`mission_not_allowed`, `mode_not_allowed`). `vehicle-sim-missions.toml` is the switch on. Ungraded: the upload's duration and `MISSION_COUNT` count.
- `sim-flight` samples the vehicle's metrics on a thread (`control/sim_probe.rs`) and records, per phase, refused envelopes by reason, lease losses, the uplink's queue delay and the adaptation's steps, and every mode change with who asked for it and the autopilot's `STATUSTEXT`. A Brake that began before the check stopped the stream is incomplete, never a pass. Never widen a budget or lifetime to make a run pass; read `readings` for the cause.
- GStreamer only through `sat-teleop-media`'s receiver, never directly; the crate therefore builds only in the build container (`just check`).

## Checks

`just gate-fast <scenario> <profile>` runs one of these per fix and `just gate-full` the per-task series (`docs/development.md`, "Gates"). The full list:

```sh
just check
just lab-up && just lab-check boot lan && just lab-check skeleton lan && just lab-check skeleton geo
just lab-check mavlink lan && just lab-check mavlink geo
just lab-check control-freshness lan && just lab-check control-freshness geo && just lab-check control-freshness outage-10s
just lab-check qgc-status lan && just lab-check qgc-status geo && just lab-check qgc-status outage-10s
just lab-check adaptation bandwidth-steps && just lab-check adaptation lte-bad --duration-s 120
just lab-check recording lan
just lab-check retrieval geo && just lab-check retrieval bandwidth-steps && just lab-check retrieval outage-10s
just ingest-check lan
just sim-check lan && just lab-check sim-flight geo
```

The agent needs `NET_ADMIN` and Linux `tc`/`nft`; the compose testbed provides both.
